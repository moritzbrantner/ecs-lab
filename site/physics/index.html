3D physics demo

Forty-eight dynamic bodies move on all three axes inside a fixed floor, ceiling, side, front, and back room. Rust sweeps fixed and moving AABB pairs through one X/Y/Z/time timeline, collects every pair and tied axis at the earliest exact impact time, resolves that contact set deterministically, and continues through the remaining timestep. The browser renders the authoritative Rust frames at a 60 FPS playback cadence and exposes direct camera controls.

Dense canonical 3D material scenario

Forty-eight bodies collide through a Rust-owned 3D room

The scene is a deterministic stress fixture rather than three showcase cubes. Bodies start across twelve spatial cells with varied sizes, masses, restitution, friction, and X/Y/Z velocity. Six fixed slabs close the room. Relative-motion swept AABB tests put body-body and body-wall impacts on the same continuous timeline, while equal-time pairs and tied axes are resolved together under a bounded deterministic contact policy.

Ownership boundary

Continuous collision time belongs to Rust, not the visualization

Rust owns relative-motion swept pair tests, exact fractional TOI comparison, global earliest contact-set collection, bounded coupled response, deterministic fixed-point quantization, and ECS writes. The Wasm adapter exports authoritative integer frames. Mouse input changes only the camera; playback and manual step selection only choose or interpolate those frames and never integrate velocity, resolve contacts, or change solver time.
